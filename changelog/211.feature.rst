Added a `sunpy.net.Fido` client, `sunkit_instruments.swpc.SWPCEventsClient`, and parsers,
`sunkit_instruments.swpc.parse_swpc_events`, `sunkit_instruments.swpc.parse_swpc_event_file` and `sunkit_instruments.swpc.parse_swpc_event_archive`,
for the NOAA SWPC Edited Solar Events List.
An example comparing the parsed events to the equivalent flare list in the HEK has also been added.
